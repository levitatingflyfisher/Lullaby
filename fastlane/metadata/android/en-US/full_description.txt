Lullaby is a baby tracker for exhausted parents that keeps everything on your device. No accounts, no cloud, no ads, no tracking.

Track it all in one place:
• Feeding — breast, bottle and solids, with a live timer and left/right toggle
• Sleep — naps and nights, start/stop
• Diapers — wet, dirty, mixed, with stool colour
• Growth — weight, height and head circumference on WHO percentile curves
• Health — medicine doses and vaccination records
• Statistics — feeding trends, sleep patterns, diaper frequency
• Timeline and calendar — browse by day or scroll the history
• Doctor summary — an at-a-glance report you can hand over as PDF or CSV

More:
• Encrypted backup with a 12-word recovery phrase
• Home-screen widget — last feed, sleep and diaper, with a live timer
• Several babies, if you have several babies
• Dark mode and Material You

The APK asks for no network permission at all, so it cannot send your child's data anywhere.

Free and open source, MIT licensed.
