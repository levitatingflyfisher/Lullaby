Lullaby is a baby tracker for exhausted parents that keeps everything on your device. No accounts, no cloud, no ads, no tracking. Two parents can share one record between their phones, sealed with your recovery words, if you choose to.

Track it all in one place:
• Feeding — breast, bottle and solids, with a live timer and left/right toggle
• Sleep — naps and nights, start/stop
• Diapers — wet, dirty, mixed, with stool colour
• Growth — weight, height and head circumference on WHO percentile curves
• Health — medicine doses and vaccination records
• Statistics — feeding trends, sleep patterns, diaper frequency
• Timeline and calendar — browse by day or scroll the history
• Doctor summary — an at-a-glance report you can hand over as PDF or CSV

More:
• Encrypted backup with a 12-word recovery phrase
• Sync with your partner's phone (optional): changes travel sealed with your recovery words, straight to the other phone on the same Wi-Fi, or through a relay you choose, which cannot read them
• A note for the next shift, on the home screen
• Home-screen widget — last feed, sleep and diaper, with a live timer
• Several babies, if you have several babies
• Dark mode and Material You

The only permission is network access, used only for sync after you turn it on: to the relay you chose, or to your partner's phone on the same Wi-Fi while you show or type a code. With sync off, the app makes no network requests.
